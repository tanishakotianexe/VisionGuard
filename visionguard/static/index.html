NEXT STEPS
Further enhancements

  

    
01
Product-specific training
Build a carefully labelled dataset of genuine and manipulated product photographs, then fine-tune and validate the detector on those images.

    
02
Explainable predictions
Add visual explanations such as Grad-CAM heatmaps so users can see which image regions influenced a model prediction.

    
03
Stronger evaluation
Test across different cameras, lighting, compression levels and AI generators; report class-wise metrics and confidence calibration.

    
04
Safer product verification
Combine image signals with OCR, barcode or QR checks, trusted product records and provenance data. Do not treat one model score as proof of authenticity.

    
05
Production readiness
Add secure uploads, file-size limits, privacy-aware retention, monitoring, model versioning and a stable hosted API.

    
06
Broader model coverage
Evaluate newer synthetic-image detectors and ensemble approaches, while checking performance on unseen datasets to reduce overfitting.

  

  
CONCLUSION
One tool, multiple verification signals.
VisionGuard brings together image classification, text extraction and product-catalog matching in a single workflow. Its current model provides an experimental signal for distinguishing real from AI-generated images, while OCR and fuzzy matching help compare visible label text with reference product data. The system is a decision-support prototype—not a guarantee of product authenticity. Product-specific validation, transparent evaluation and trusted external evidence are essential before real-world use.
